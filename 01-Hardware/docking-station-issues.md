Dell Pro Dock – WD25: Docking Station Not Powering On

    Check the power adapter

        Make sure the Dell dock power adapter is firmly connected to the dock and wall socket.

        Check whether the power LED on the dock is ON.

    Check the power outlet

        Test the wall socket with another device.

        If using a power strip/UPS, try connecting the dock directly to the wall outlet.

    Check the power cable

        Inspect the adapter and cable for cuts, bends, or loose connections.

        Reseat both ends of the power cable.

    Power-cycle the dock

        Disconnect the dock's power adapter.

        Disconnect the USB-C cable from the laptop.

        Wait 30 seconds, reconnect power, then reconnect the laptop.

    Check the laptop connection

        Ensure the dock's USB-C/Thunderbolt cable is properly connected to the laptop.

        Try another compatible USB-C port if available.

    If still no power

        If the adapter/outlet is working but the dock has no power LED, suspect a faulty power adapter or dock hardware.

        Replace/test with a known-good Dell-compatible adapter or raise a hardware service ticket.
